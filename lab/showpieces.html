pobv.dev · component lab

Interactive showpieces

Four candidate "wow" components, each a self-contained vanilla Canvas 2D module (no libraries, no build step) matching the site's paper + blue language. Move your cursor over each stage. Each lives in its own file under /lab/ and can be dropped into any page: Lab.name(canvasEl, opts). Every module auto-pauses when off-screen and honors reduced-motion.

01

Fluid ink

drag to swirl

A real fluid simulation (Stam stable-fluids). Drag to inject velocity and dye; the ink swirls, advects, and slowly settles back into paper.

lab/fluid-ink.js · Lab.fluidInk(el,{n,dt,fade,ink})

02

Flowing silk

move to part the stream

Thousands of particles trace a value-noise flow field, leaving fading trails so the motion reads as flowing silk. Your cursor repels them and the stream parts around it.

lab/flow-field.js · Lab.flowField(el,{count,scale,speed})

03

Magnetic field

move to warp the mesh

A grid of dots joined by fine lines. The cursor is a gravity well: nearby dots slide toward it and swell, the mesh stretching like liquid metal, then springing back.

lab/magnetic-grid.js · Lab.magneticGrid(el,{gap,range,pull})

04

Physics type

grab & fling the letters

Every letter is a rigid body with gravity. Grab one and throw it; they tumble, collide, and pile up. Hit Reset to drop them again.

lab/physics-type.js · Lab.physicsType(el,{text,size,color})
