Eleven hours between flights is too long to stay in the terminal and too short to do much outside it. So the plan was simple: one walk, one meal, one view.

## The walk

Out along the water in the evening, when it had cooled enough to be pleasant. The city was mostly lights and wide roads.

## The meal

> Order the thing the table next to you is eating.

It's never failed yet.

## The list for next time

1. Book the lounge earlier
2. Bring a lighter jacket for the aircon
3. Leave the laptop in the bag
