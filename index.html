Overview

§1 · /overview

I'm a Staff Software Engineer in NYC who builds clean, robust systems at scale — and who's just as invested in the people building them. I've spent a decade at the seam where good engineering meets good culture, and I'm known as a servant leader who brings the best out of a team.

My work has spanned backend engineering, distributed systems, and technical leadership at Automox, Datasite, and Target — from device-management platforms and billing systems processing billions of dollars, to pipelines ingesting petabytes, to infrastructure powering target.com, one of the top-60 most-visited sites in the US.

Outside of code I'm endlessly curious about the world. I've traveled to ~80 countries, I'm fluent in Thai and English, and I'm always learning. I love developing people — I've coached interns and junior engineers through code, career, and everything in between, watching them grow into confident seniors. Above all, I try to show up with kindness and thoughtfulness, in code and in life.
