Personal recommendations from someone who grew up there. No tourist traps, just the good stuff — and where something isn't worth your time, I say so.

Start with when to go. Thailand punishes that choice harder than most countries: the two coasts have opposite rainy seasons, and the north's high season is also its worst air of the year.

Everything below this line is about places. This part is about dates, and it decides more of your trip than the itinerary does. Get the month right first, then pick the region — not the other way round.

หมอกควันภาคเหนือ Haze, the north Feb – Apr Agricultural burning. Avoid Chiang Mai / Chiang Rai.

The trap: burning season is high season

Feb–Apr looks perfect on a weather chart and sits inside the expensive months, but it is when the north's air is genuinely bad. It is the single biggest planning mistake people make with Chiang Mai and Chiang Rai. Check the AQI for those dates before you book anything.

Don't book both coasts blindly

The Andaman and the Gulf have opposite rainy seasons. There is no month that is good for both, so one coast per trip — and let the month you can travel pick which one.

สงกรานต์ Songkran
13–15 April. A nationwide water fight — the whole country, in the street, for three days. Either plan around it or plan into it, but don't be surprised by it.
ลอยกระทง Loy Krathong / ยี่เป็ง Yi Peng
November. Lanterns and floated offerings, biggest in Chiang Mai. The date moves every year with the lunar calendar, so look it up before you fix flights.

เรื่องควรรู้ Logistics

Five things worth sorting before you land.

  1. Grab The rideshare and food-delivery app, and the one piece of software you actually need in Thailand. Install it before you go.
  2. A tourist SIM or eSIM Cheap at the airport and worth it immediately — Grab and maps are both useless without data.
  3. Sleeper trains Overnight trains are a real option, not a novelty: Bangkok–Chiang Mai for the north, Bangkok–Surat Thani for the south. They book out early.
  4. Cash for national parks Parks usually charge a separate foreigner price, and card readers there are unreliable. Carry baht.
  5. Your visa-free stay Check the current length for your passport before the trip. It has changed more than once recently, so don't go on what a blog post says.

ภาคเหนือ

The North 7 places

Mountains and forests. เชียงใหม่ Chiang Mai and เชียงราย Chiang Rai are the two major cities and the natural bases to sleep in. But if you can find a legitimate place to stay near Phu Chi Fa — do that instead.

ดอย doi — a mountain peak. It prefixes half the names up here.

Not February to April

This is the region burning season ruins. Everything below is worth the trip, and none of it is worth doing through the haze — see the year.

  1. ภูชี้ฟ้าPhu Chi Fa

    ห้ามพลาด Must-do

    You climb high enough to be above the clouds. At sunrise you are looking out over a sea of cloud — one of the most surreal sights in Thailand.

    When
    Sunrise
    Plan
    Arrive the night before
    Base
    Chiang Rai

  2. วัดร่องขุ่นWat Rong Khun

    ห้ามพลาด Must-do

    The White Temple. All-white architecture with mirror inlays — extraordinarily beautiful, and famous with Thai and foreign visitors alike. Do not skip this.

    Base
    Chiang Rai

  3. บ้านดำBaan Dam — the Black House

    แนะนำ Recommended

    The dark counterpart to the White Temple, by a different Chiang Rai artist. People usually do the two of them as a pair, and they are better that way — the contrast is the point.

    Pair with
    Wat Rong Khun
    Base
    Chiang Rai

  4. สามเหลี่ยมทองคำThe Golden Triangle

    แนะนำ Recommended

    Where Thailand, Laos and Burma converge — you can stand at the border of three countries at once, and it is a neat place to cross into either one.

    Plenty of tourists, which means plenty of accommodation. Don't let the crowds put you off; it is genuinely nice.

  5. ดอยตุงDoi Tung

    แนะนำ Recommended

    Beautiful gardens built to honour the King's mother. Worth the trip for the scenery alone.

    Base
    Chiang Rai

  6. ดอยแม่สลองDoi Mae Salong

    ถ้ามีเวลา If you have time

    A Yunnanese Chinese hill town surrounded by tea plantations — a different culture and a different kitchen from anywhere else on this list. Close enough to fold into the Phu Chi Fa / Golden Triangle run rather than make a trip of it.

    Pair with
    Phu Chi Fa, Golden Triangle
    Base
    Chiang Rai

  7. ดอยอินทนนท์Doi Inthanon

    ถ้ามีเวลา If you have time

    The highest point in Thailand. Near the top are two large เจดีย์ chedi built for the King's and the Queen's 60th birthdays. Worth it if the days allow, but it is not the priority.

    Base
    Chiang Mai

ภาคใต้

The South 7 places

The most beautiful beaches are along the Andaman Coast — ตรัง Trang, กระบี่ Krabi, and the islands scattered off them. That is the side I would send you to, and Koh Lanta is the base to do it from.

เกาะ koh (also spelled ko) — island. Everything below with a “Koh” is one.

One coast per trip — the south is really two trips

The Andaman side and the Gulf side have opposite rainy seasons, so no month is good for both. Andaman: Nov–Apr. Gulf: Jun–Sep. Pick the coast that matches the month you can actually travel, then book inside that half of this section — the year is charted up top.

ฝั่งอันดามัน Andaman side

West coast · Nov – Apr

  1. เกาะลันตาKoh Lanta

    ห้ามพลาด Must-do

    Popular with tourists but genuinely beautiful, with a real range of places to stay — luxury hotels down to cheap small rooms. This is the base: everything below is a day tour from here.

    Role
    Your base in the south
    • เกาะรอก Koh Rok — one of the most beautiful beaches available. Barely any buildings, well preserved, and you can camp there. If you book an island tour, make sure Koh Rok is on it.
    • เกาะมุก Koh Mook — an emerald green beach inside a cave. Absolutely amazing.
    • Snorkelling and diving — it feels like a different world. I swam with a school of thousands of fish.
    • Sea cave tours — worth a morning.
    • Waterfalls — seasonal, and best during the rainy season.

  2. เขาสกKhao Sok National Park

    ห้ามพลาด Must-do

    Not an island — เชี่ยวหลาน Cheow Lan lake, with floating raft houses sitting under limestone karsts. Often the best-rated thing in the whole south that isn't a beach.

    Go and look at images of it, then book ahead. The raft houses fill up, and this is not somewhere you turn up and sort out on the day.

    Plan
    Book well ahead
    Stay
    Floating raft house

  3. เกาะพีพีKoh Phi Phi

    แนะนำ Recommended

    Another popular one, and still good. More upscale and more developed than Koh Lanta — genuinely beautiful, just pricier.

  4. อ่าวพังงาAo Phang Nga National Park

    แนะนำ Recommended

    Home to เกาะตะปู Koh Tapu — James Bond Island, from The Man with the Golden Gun. The limestone karsts rising straight out of the water are the iconic ones.

ภูเก็ต Phuket

ข้าม Skip it

A tourist trap with a huge party scene. Nothing there is authentically Thai, the beaches are subpar and the prices are inflated. Unless you are specifically after clubs and raves, everything above is a better use of the same days.

ฝั่งอ่าวไทย Gulf side

East coast · Jun – Sep

The other half of the south, and the answer if you are travelling in the months the Andaman side is wet. Nothing here displaces Koh Lanta for me — it is the coast you go to because of when you can go.

  1. เกาะเต่าKoh Tao

    แนะนำ Recommended

    The cheap-diving island. Busy, but that is because it is where the certifications happen — if you want to come home with an open-water licence, this is where you get it.

    Go for
    Dive certification

  2. หมู่เกาะอ่างทองAng Thong Marine Park

    แนะนำ Recommended

    The archipelago sitting off the Samui / Phangan cluster — the scenery payoff of this coast, and the reason to be based there at all.

    From
    Koh Samui

  3. เกาะสมุยKoh Samui

    ถ้ามีเวลา If you have time

    Easy to get to — it has its own airport — but it gets very busy. Useful mostly as the gateway to Ang Thong and the rest of the cluster.

    Role
    Gateway to the Gulf islands

Either coast: plenty of the smaller islands are beautiful too, but remote and inconvenient — more adventurous than relaxing. Many of them have campgrounds if that is your style.

ภาคกลาง

Central — Bangkok 10 places

กรุงเทพฯ Bangkok is where you will land and where you will leave from, so give it real days rather than the leftovers. Three of those days can leave the city entirely — the day trips are at the bottom of this section.

วัด wat — a Buddhist temple. The big ones enforce covered shoulders and knees.

  1. วัดพระแก้วWat Phra Kaew & the Grand Palace

    ห้ามพลาด Must-do

    Go to all the famous temples, but prioritise this one. The พระบรมมหาราชวัง palace complex is stunning — and it is beautiful again, differently, lit up at night.

    When
    Also worth seeing after dark
    Dress
    Shoulders and knees covered

  2. วัดอรุณ · วัดโพธิ์Wat Arun & Wat Pho

    แนะนำ Recommended

    The other two on the river. Wat Arun is best at sunset, seen from the ธนบุรี Thonburi side; Wat Pho holds the reclining Buddha. Both pair with Wat Phra Kaew, and the three of them make one clean river day.

    When
    Wat Arun at sunset
    Plan
    One river day, all three

  3. เยาวราชYaowarat — Chinatown

    แนะนำ Recommended

    Go after dark. The street-food stretch is the reason — the whole road turns over into stalls once the sun is down, and it is the single best eating in the city.

    When
    After dark

  4. ล่องเรือเจ้าพระยาThe river at night, from ICON Siam

    แนะนำ Recommended

    Take a boat from ไอคอนสยาม ICON Siam at night and go around the city on the เจ้าพระยา Chao Phraya. The views are amazing, and it is the cheapest way to see Bangkok look expensive.

    When
    Night
    From
    ICON Siam pier

  5. สยามSiam

    แนะนำ Recommended

    The heart of modern Bangkok. Siam Paragon and Central World are each bigger than the Mall of America, and they are one block apart. Siam Discovery and Siam Centre sit alongside them and skew younger and trendier.

  6. บ้านจิม ทอมป์สันJim Thompson House

    ถ้ามีเวลา If you have time

    Old Thai teak houses reassembled into one compound, the silk business that made them, and the disappearance story that ends it. A short, contained visit and a good one on a hot afternoon.

  7. พิพิธภัณฑ์ MOCAMOCA Bangkok

    ถ้ามีเวลา If you have time

    A genuinely nice modern art museum, and the Thai work in it is far more interesting than most visitors expect. Worth a half day if museums are your thing.

ทริปวันเดียว Day trips out of Bangkok

All doable and back the same night

  1. ตลาดน้ำอัมพวา · ตลาดร่มหุบAmphawa & the Maeklong Railway Market

    แนะนำ Recommended

    About an hour south of Bangkok. Of all the floating markets, Amphawa is the one I would send you to — the liveliest, with the most to see. It gets extremely crowded, and that is the trade.

    The Maeklong Railway Market is the one that folds itself up when the train comes through and unfolds again behind it. The two are usually bundled together, they fit in a single day, and I recommend doing them that way.

    From
    1 hour south of Bangkok
    Plan
    Both in one day
    • ตลาดน้ำท่าคา Tha Kha — if Amphawa sounds too crowded. Smaller and more local.
    • คลองลัดมะยม Khlong Lat Mayom — inside Bangkok itself, and food-focused. The option that costs you no travel day at all.

  2. อยุธยาAyutthaya

    แนะนำ Recommended

    The ruined old capital, an hour and a bit from Bangkok and an easy day trip by train or on a tour. Still genuinely unique and worth the day.

    Be ready for the heat — it is open ground and there is very little shade.

    From
    1 hr+ by train or tour
    Bring
    Sun cover and water

  3. เขาใหญ่Khao Yai National Park

    ถ้ามีเวลา If you have time

    The closest real national park to Bangkok — wildlife and waterfalls, and the easiest way to get actual forest into a city-based trip.

    Bring
    Cash for the park fee

มารยาท

Manners 8 rules

None of these are suggestions. The first four will genuinely offend people, and one of them is a criminal offence.

  1. อย่าแตะหัวใคร Don't touch anyone's head or hair

    The head is considered the most sacred part of the body.

  2. อย่าเหยียบเงินหรือหนังสือ Don't step on money or books

    Feet are the lowest part of the body, so putting one on anything is an insult to it.

  3. อย่าวิจารณ์สถาบันพระมหากษัตริย์ Don't criticise the monarchy

    This is literally illegal under Thai law. It is not a joke and it is not a debate to have in public.

  4. อย่าแตะพระพุทธรูป Don't touch any Buddhist sculpture

    Even seemingly casual contact is deeply disrespectful.

  5. อย่าประมาทเรื่องทรัพย์สิน Don't be careless with your things

    Know where your bag, phone and wallet are at all times, particularly in crowds and markets.

  6. เก็บกระเป๋าสตางค์ไว้กระเป๋าหน้า Keep your wallet in your front pocket

    Never the back pocket, even if you think you are careful. No one there does it.

  7. แต่งกายสุภาพเมื่อเข้าวัด Cover your shoulders and knees at temples

    And take your shoes off. The big temples enforce it, and they will turn you away at the gate.

  8. ยืนตรงเคารพเพลงชาติ Stand for the national anthem

    It plays at 8am and 6pm every day. Take your hat off. Everyone around you stops what they are doing.

Pick the month, then the coast. Then string it north to south and finish in Bangkok — that is the order I would travel it in.
