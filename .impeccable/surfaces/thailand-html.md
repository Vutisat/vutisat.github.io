# Surface brief — thailand.html

Scope: `thailand.html` only. Visitor mode: **Read**. Leaves the shared `main.css`
"Reference" world; `life.html` and `resume.html` keep it untouched.

Audience: a friend actually planning a Thailand trip, arriving from the homepage
secret menu or a direct link. Job: decide regions, what to book, what to skip, in
what order. Content is restructured and expanded from `docs/Thailand_recs.txt` —
nothing invented. Thai script sits beside English as real bilingual labels.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A garland is a strung sequence you receive as an offering, so the whole
guide hangs from one literal thread and what the reader is given is a route, not a
list. It refuses the category's card grid over full-bleed photography.

**OWN-WORLD:** Jasmine off-white ground `#FBF7EF`; one Vanda-orchid purple thread
`#6B3FA0` / `#4A2472` that is the only continuous line on the page; marigold gold
`#E0A310` for tier bands and verdict marks; banana-leaf green `#2E6B4F` scarce.
Buds, knots, tags and tiers are the component family — no cards, no shadows, no
photographs. Recognizable with all content removed by the thread alone.

**STORY:** The reader understands Thailand as three regions strung in order,
believes the verdicts because they are first-hand and unhedged (Phuket is skipped
out loud), and leaves with a route they can book against.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Near-empty jasmine ground. One purple thread drops from the top
edge holding the title as a tag — เที่ยวไทย over THAILAND, พบ แนะนำ beneath. The
thread continues down; the first tier gathers around it — เหนือ THE NORTH as a
marigold band knotted onto the string — then ภูชี้ฟ้า as the first bud with its
facts on the tag hanging from it. The thread never leaves the page; it is the
scroll position. Primary action for a planner is the tier index knotted to the
thread, reachable from the first screen.

**FORM:** พวงมาลัย — the orchid garland. Candidate 4 of the re-rolled grounded list,
chosen by the user in the safer register against Benjarong and Lai Rot Nam; the
purple-and-gold, orchid, Thai-art palette is user-pinned and beats the roll. Seed
key `0715ed58`, re-roll round 1, register safer, code-led.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
