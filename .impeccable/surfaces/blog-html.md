Mode: Read. Audience: friends, peers, recruiters reading longer writing. Job: a hand-editable blog with no build step: Markdown files in posts/, a one-line manifest per post, an index and a reader. Posts shipped are SAMPLE (one doubles as the how-to). User AFK; assigned direction taken without a decision page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each post is a pocket memo book (kraft cover, rubber-stamped date and place); reading it opens the book to ruled pages. Refuses the card-grid blog index.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft board #C9A57A covers with a printed rule and corner rounds, graph and ruled paper #FBFAF4 pages in blue-grey rules, red rubber-stamp ink for dates, margin notes in the outer gutter; Jost for covers and stamps, Literata for reading.

STORY: Visitors see the row of notebooks, newest first, pick one, read it like a field notebook, flip to the next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Index: the title stamped on a wide kraft band, then the row of memo books standing on a desk, newest largest. Reader: the opened book, cover flap at left, ruled page right.

FORM: Pocket memo books, candidate 3 of 7, seed ccb61a68. Raise (from daylight section): every entry carries a precise date and place stamp; annotations pinned in the margin.
