Pob & Katy's Singapore Adventure

Thursday arrival, 11:20 am  ·  Sunday departure, 9:40pm
4
days
Day 1 Thursday Marina Bay & arrival
11:20 am
Land & check in
Arrive at Changi, transfer to hotel, drop bags and freshen up.
+ Getting around

Grab (rideshare) door-to-door, or the MRT — fast, spotless and cheap; tap in with a contactless card or phone (SimplyGo). Changi ↔ city is ~20–30 min by Grab (~S$20–30).

Late afternoon
Merlion Park
Singapore's iconic mascot statue — quick photo stop, free, open 24/7.
+ Photo spot — the classic Merlion shot

Stand across the promenade and line the Merlion up “spouting” into the Marina Bay Sands backdrop. Free and open 24/7 — golden hour here is gorgeous.

Sunset
Marina Bay Sands SkyPark
Signature skyline view as day turns to night — book tickets in advance.
+ Photo spot — Helix Bridge, blue hour

Just below the SkyPark: the double-helix bridge frames the MBS + skyline reflection. Shoot around 7pm as the lights come up for the postcard shot.

+ For two — a sunset drink

CÉ LA VI sits right on top of Marina Bay Sands; Lantern at Fullerton Bay Hotel is a lower-key rooftop right over the water.

Evening
Lau Pa SatFOOD
Dinner option — famous for Satay Street at night, skewers grilled right at your table.
+ Alternative — chilli crab

If you’d rather a sit-down seafood dinner, Jumbo Seafood at Clarke Quay does the national dish — order fried mantou buns to mop up the sauce.

8 / 9pm
Spectra show
Free light & water show right outside Marina Bay Sands.
+ Photo tip — best viewpoint

Watch from the Event Plaza steps outside the MBS shops for the full water-and-light spread; arrive ~10 min early for a front-row spot.

Day 2 Friday Gardens by the Bay
Morning
Cloud Forest
Gardens by the Bay — indoor waterfall & misty rainforest dome.
+ Photo spot — the indoor waterfall

The 35m waterfall is the money shot — go right at opening (9am) to beat both the crowds and the lens-fogging mist.

+ Before you go — kaya toast

Fuel up the local way: coconut-jam toast, soft-boiled eggs and kopi at Ya Kun or Killiney. (kopi = coffee w/ condensed milk; add kosong for no sugar.)

Late morning
Flower Dome
Cooler dome with Mediterranean-climate flowers.
Early afternoon
Satay by the BayFOOD
Food court right by Gardens by the Bay — good hawker variety without leaving the area.
Afternoon
Supertree Grove
The iconic giant tree structures — great photos.
+ Photo spot — OCBC Skyway

Walk the 22m aerial walkway strung between the Supertrees for the aerial row shot; cheap tickets, open till ~9pm — time it with Garden Rhapsody below.

7:45 or 8:45pm
Garden Rhapsody
Free nightly light & sound show at the Supertrees.
+ Photo tip — lie back for it

Lie on the ground near the center of the grove and shoot straight up as the Supertrees light up in sync with the music.

Day 3 Saturday Gardens & Civic District
Morning
Singapore Botanic Gardens
UNESCO World Heritage park — shaded paths and lakes, free entry.
+ Photo spot — Swan Lake & Bandstand

The white Victorian bandstand on its little hill and the Swan Lake gazebo are the garden’s prettiest, quietest frames early in the morning.

Late morning
National Orchid Garden
Thousands of orchid varieties inside the Botanic Gardens — paid entry.
+ Photo spot — the orchid arches

The Golden Shower archway and the cool VIP Orchid house are the standouts — endless colour, best mid-morning before it fills up.

Early afternoon
Lunch near Bras BasahFOOD
Casual local chain near the Civic District — good hainanese chicken rice.
Afternoon
National Gallery Singapore
Art museum in the restored former City Hall / Supreme Court.
+ Photo spot — Old Hill Street shutters

A 5-min walk away: the rainbow-shuttered facade of the old Hill Street Police Station — one of Singapore’s most photographed spots, best in soft light.

+ Photo spot — Fort Canning tree tunnel

Also nearby: the spiral staircase framed by a canopy “tree tunnel” in Fort Canning Park. Quietest earlier in the day.

Evening
Free evening
Rest, a casual walk, or hangout with Marvin.
+ Hidden gem — Henderson Waves at sunset

A sculptural wave-form bridge on the Southern Ridges forest walk — beautiful at golden hour and a peaceful way to end the day.

+ Hidden gem — Tiong Bahru

Or Singapore’s coolest old quarter: Art-Deco walk-ups, murals, Tiong Bahru Bakery and indie shops, with very few tourists.

Day 4 Sunday Kampong Glam, Chinatown & departure
Morning
Kampong Glam
Sultan Mosque, Haji Lane murals and cafes — compact, quick to explore.
+ Photo spot — Haji Lane & Sultan Mosque

Shoot Haji Lane’s murals and pastel shophouses, then frame the golden-domed Sultan Mosque straight down Bussorah Street — you’re here in good morning light.

~11am
Hill Street Tai Hwa Pork NoodleFOOD
Singapore's only current Michelin-starred hawker stall — bak chor mee noodles, ~S$9 (expect a long queue).
Midday
Chinatown
Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, shophouse streets.
+ Photo spot — Buddha Tooth Relic Temple

The ornate red-and-gold temple on South Bridge Road, plus the lantern-strung shophouse lanes (Pagoda & Trengganu Streets).

Early afternoon
Liao Fan Soya Sauce Chicken Rice & Noodle @ MaxwellFOOD
Hawker Chan's soy sauce chicken (former Michelin star, still cheap and iconic) — skip if full.
+ Also at Maxwell — Tian Tian

While you’re in this hawker centre, Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice is the other legend here — grab a plate before the queue builds.

~5–5:30pm
Head to Changi
Leave time for Jewel before your 9:40pm flight.
+ If you’re ahead — Katong detour

A quick detour east: Koon Seng Road’s rainbow Peranakan houses and 328 Katong Laksa. Only if you’re comfortably early for the airport.

Pre-flight
Jewel Changi Airport
Rain Vortex waterfall + Canopy Park.
+ Photo spot — the Rain Vortex

The world’s tallest indoor waterfall; the evening light & sound show (on the hour) is the shot. Leave a buffer before your 9:40pm flight.
