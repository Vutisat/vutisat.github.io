Heater

  • It's beside the bed, by the window. Unfortunately it's only ON or OFF.
  • To switch it, twist the valve all the way until it won't go any further.
  • If it gets too hot, turn it off and repeat. You can also crack the window a little (but that comes with noise).
  • Or keep it off and use the space heater instead.

AC

  • The remote is there. Feel free to use fan mode.
Please don't run the AC when it's below about 68° outside. It damages the unit.

House Rules & Info

  1. There's a fire extinguisher under the sink.
  2. PLEASE PLEASE don't forget to turn off the gas stovetop after using it. It can be dangerous.
  3. No open flame in the apartment, candles included.
  4. Be careful with the toaster oven! It has caught fire in the past.
    1. I don't recommend running it more than ~10 minutes at a time, and keep an eye on it.
    2. Don't go above 400°.
    3. 5-minute intervals should be no problem.
    4. If it catches fire: keep it closed and unplug it. See if it goes out on its own. Stand by with the extinguisher.
  5. No drugs or smoking. Smoking will set off the alarm, and it's illegal indoors anyway.
  6. No shoes on my white rug.
  7. Feel free to read my books, but please be careful with them.
    1. Some are signed or have sentimental value.
    2. The rougher used ones I care a lot less about.
    3. If you take one out of the apartment, I'd appreciate you letting me know which title.
    4. Search the catalog: vutisat.github.io/library.html
  8. Feel free to play any of my board games.
  9. Please be careful with the monitor.
    1. If something gets on it or you want to clean it, do it as gently as possible, with a microfiber cloth only.
    2. It's the latest OLED tech, so it's far more sensitive than a regular screen. A paper towel or ordinary cloth will damage it. (I'm not trying to be anal 😅)
  10. Please be mindful of noise.
    1. NYC walls can be thin, and it's a shared building.
    2. Before midnight, being a bit loud is usually no big deal.
