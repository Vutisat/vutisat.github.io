The book

Why this one

Steinbeck's big California novel, set in the Salinas Valley and following a couple of families across several generations. It's about 600 pages, which is exactly why it's nicer with company: long enough to live with for a few months, and full of things you'll want to talk through with someone.

No need to have read any Steinbeck before, and no homework beyond the reading. Come as you are.

How it works

A few house rules

I

No spoilers, ever

On calls and in the group chat, we only talk about what's been assigned so far. Reading ahead is totally fine, just keep it to yourself.

II

Skip the introduction

A lot of editions open with an essay that gives away the story. Save it, and any online summaries or reading guides, for after you finish.

III

Go by chapters

Page numbers differ between editions, so every checkpoint is a chapter number. Paperback, ebook, library copy and audiobook all count.

IV

Behind? Still come

Life happens. If you're a few chapters short, join anyway. You'll just hear a little more than you've read.
